Welcome!

Parents, here’s how the study will go:

  • Your child will hear two short stories and answer some questions about them.
  • Some questions will ask your child to answer “yes” or “no,” or to choose between two options. These choices will appear as buttons on the screen. If your child can press the buttons, please let them do so. If they can’t, please listen to their answer and press the button that matches what they said.
  • Other questions are open-ended. Let your child press the “Next” button after they are done answering (or press “Next” for them.)
  • Please stay with your child for the whole study. If your child can interact with the screens on their own, you don’t need to remain in view of the webcam the entire time, but please stay nearby to help with any technical issues. Your child does need to remain in view of the webcam for the whole study as a condition for receiving compensation.
  • Remember: There are no wrong answers! We are interested in how children think about these situations, which may well be different from how adults think about them. We are not judging your child, and we are not evaluating you as a parent.
    • It is important not to answer for the child or give away any of your own thoughts about the answers. It’s often best not to say anything at all while your child is thinking and answering. If you feel that would be too awkward, you may say something neutral from time to time (but not “Right!” or “Good job!”)
    • If your child doesn’t answer a question right away, let them think about it a bit, then encourage them to answer in their own words.
  • This is a test run (pilot) of the study, so you and your child can help us improve it! Please be on the lookout for anything that seems unclear or makes it hard for your child to tell us what they think. You’ll have a chance to share any such observations with us in an (optional) exit survey.
  • Now, we’d love to speak with your child!
