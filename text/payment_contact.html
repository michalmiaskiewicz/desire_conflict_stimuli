Thank you

To thank you for your participation, we will email you a $5 Amazon gift card within one week. To receive the gift card, you must (1) live in the United States and (2) have provided a valid consent statement. Your child must (3) be in the age range for the study, (4) be visible on the webcam during the study, and (5) have answered questions in English. Additionally, (6) this must be the first time that your child participated in this study.

Staying in touch

This study is part of a broader inquiry into parent-child “desire conflicts” – situations in which what a child wants conflicts with what a parent wants. We are interested in how such conflicts arise, how parents and children understand and experience them, and how they are resolved. If you have thoughts about these issues – or any further questions or feedback about this study – we’d be very happy to hear from you at mmiaskiewicz@g.harvard.edu.
