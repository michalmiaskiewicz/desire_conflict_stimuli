Why we are running this study

Thank you very much for your time and for taking part in this study with your child!

With this study, we are trying to understand how children evaluate situations where what they want to do interferes with a parent’s ongoing activity. Many things children want to do at home are not wrong in themselves, but they can conflict with what parents or other family members are trying to accomplish.

We are studying whether children of various ages think it is wrong (and feels at least somewhat bad) to keep doing something they want, even after their parent asks them to stop. We are especially interested in whether children think it is worse (and feels worse) to ignore a parent’s request to stop when the parent explains why the activity would disrupt them, compared to when the parent gives no explanation. For this reason, your child may have heard stories in which the mother did or did not explain why she was asking the child to stop.

Request for feedback

This session was a pilot (test run) of the study, and we are very grateful that you and your child were among its first participants! Your child’s responses and behavior will give us invaluable clues about what we should change or improve for future participants.

There are some things, however, that only you can tell us because you know your child best. If you’d like, please let us know in the text box below:

  • How easy or difficult was it for your child to do this study?
  • Was there anything that was unclear or may have interfered with your child’s ability to understand the stories or answer the questions truthfully?
  • Did you experience any technical difficulties?
